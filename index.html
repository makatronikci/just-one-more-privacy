Privacy Policy

Just One More is a casual mobile game developed by makatronikci.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use the mobile game Just One More (the "App").

The App is developed and published by makatronikci.

1. Information We Collect

Just One More does not require you to create an account and does not directly ask you to provide personal information such as your name, phone number, contacts, photographs, or precise location.

However, the App uses third-party advertising services. These services may automatically process certain information from your device in order to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertisements.

Depending on your device, region, advertising settings, and consent choices, information processed by advertising services may include:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device and app information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Advertising and interaction information
  • Diagnostic and performance information

The exact information processed may vary depending on your device settings, location, consent status, and applicable third-party policies.

2. Advertising

Just One More uses Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App.

Google AdMob may process information to provide and measure advertisements, prevent fraud and abuse, and support advertising services.

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Just One More uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent relating to advertising and other applicable purposes.

Depending on your location and applicable legal requirements, you may be shown a consent request when using the App.

Where required, privacy choices can also be accessed from the Privacy Options section within the App's Settings screen.

You may make choices about how your information is used for advertising purposes through the consent and privacy controls presented by the App.

4. Local Game Data

Just One More may store certain game-related information locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Your best score
  • Game settings such as sound preferences
  • Other locally stored game configuration data

This information is stored locally to provide the functionality of the App and is not directly submitted by Just One More to a server operated by the developer.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Retention

Just One More does not operate its own user account system or personal-information database.

Game-related information stored locally on your device remains there until it is removed by the App, the operating system, or the user.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google is retained and handled according to their respective policies and applicable legal requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

Just One More is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

8. Data Security

We do not operate our own server for collecting user accounts or personal information through Just One More.

Third-party service providers used by the App are responsible for applying appropriate security measures to information they process.
